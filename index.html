Measuring metal parts from a camera frame

I worked on a project that scans metal part outlines and bolt-hole positions from camera frames using contour tracing and binary segmentation, achieving millimetre-level dimensional accuracy through manual pixel-to-mm calibration.

Press run to scan the part.

An illustration of the technique running in your browser on a synthetic part, at a demo scale of 0.5 mm per pixel. The real project works on live camera frames with manual pixel-to-mm calibration.
